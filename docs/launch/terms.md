## 1. What SmartNutri is

SmartNutri reads packaged-food information and shows, for each family member you add, whether a product looks `ok`, needs `caution`, is `unknown` (we could not read enough to judge) or is best avoided (`avoid`). It also tracks what members eat.

**SmartNutri is information, not medical advice.**

- It is not a medical device.
- It does not diagnose, treat, manage or cure any disease.
- It does not replace a doctor or dietitian.
- Always read the product's own label, especially for allergies.

**`unknown` does not mean safe.**

## 2. Accuracy

- Verdicts depend on the label and product data, and on the profile you enter. Labels can be wrong, out of date or hard to read. Data from Open Food Facts is crowd-sourced.
- Health thresholds are based on published guidance and are pending review by a clinician.
- Check values the app reads from a photo before you save them.
- Use "Report a problem" if something looks wrong.
